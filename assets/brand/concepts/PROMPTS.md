# Logo concept prompts

These images were made with the built-in image-generation tool. Each prompt asked for an original, flat, vector-like mark on a clear background. Each mark had to stay readable at 32×32 pixels and avoid text, trademarks, Minecraft art, and the Roblox logo.

## A. Block W

Make a bold W silhouette from five simple isometric cubes. Use deep navy with one copper-orange cube. Keep a strong shape and wide clear padding.

## B. Maker pencil

Combine one simple voxel block with a carpenter's pencil. Use dark blue, teal, and a copper-orange pencil. Make the symbol feel hands-on and easy to understand.

## C. Import and merge

Show three small blocks flowing into one larger block with one bold arrow. Use navy, sky blue, and one copper-orange accent. Keep the motion clear.

## D. Maker stamp

Cut a large W shape from the top of one solid square block. Add one copper-orange square near a lower corner. Keep the mark flat, bold, and readable at 16×16 pixels.
