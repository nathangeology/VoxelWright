# Round-two logo prompts

These images were made with the built-in image-generation tool. The prompts used the rules in [the logo design brief](../../../../docs/logo-design-brief.md).

All four prompts asked for a clear background, strong light and dark contrast, about 10% clear space, no trademarks, no copied game art, and a mark that could work at 16–32 pixels.

## D3. Wrapped W cube

Build one isometric block from a continuous W-shaped ribbon. Wrap the W around the visible faces like digital joinery. Add one orange voxel in a cut corner. Keep it meaningful in one color.

## B2. Voxel pencil cut

Fuse a chunky carpenter pencil and a block into one symbol. Build the pencil from three voxel segments. Let its tip cut the center valley of a W from the block. Avoid a loose pencil placed beside a cube.

## B3. Voxel nib

Fuse a blocky W and a pencil-nib diamond into one abstract mark. Use one missing square to suggest a voxel being placed. Hint at making and editing without showing a literal pencil.

## D4. Pixel-cut stamp

Cut a stepped W fully inside a square maker's stamp. Remove one corner square and place it beside the opening as the orange voxel accent. Keep the top edge flat and the design usable in one color.
