# Finalist source prompts

Both finalist images were made with the built-in image-generation tool.

## Primary interlock mark

Create two chunky interlocking block pieces. Let their shared seam form a capital W like a dovetail joint. Add one orange voxel above the center. Use navy, teal, and orange on a clear background. Keep the shape bold enough for a plugin icon.

## Toolbar stamp

Cut a stepped W fully inside a square maker's stamp. Remove one corner square and place it beside the opening as the orange voxel accent. Keep the top edge flat. Use navy and orange on a clear background, and make the mark work in one color.
